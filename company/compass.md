# Investment thesis
Mobi is well positioned for continued growth, supported by strong value drivers

Exposure to a large and structurally growing corporate mobility market
Structural demand for corporate employee transportation is supported by clients’ operational needs, a national shortage of professional drivers, and labor regulations (NR-31) requiring compliant transport in areas without adequate public transit
01.
Solid revenue model aligned with client demand, supporting strong customer stickiness
Long-term contracts (3-5 years) with built-in annual price adjustment mechanisms (IPCA, collective bargaining agreement, and diesel price variation) protect margin and reinforce client retention
02.
Asset base that compounds competitive advantage
A modern, fully-owned fleet of 373 vehicles segregated across buses, micro-buses and vans creates operational flexibility, service quality control, and barriers that are hard for competitors to replicate
03.
Integrated capabilities that deliver a full-service edge
GPS-based telemetry and driver monitoring systems[, combined with in-house maintenance capabilities to be confirmed with management,] position Mobi as a reliable, high-uptime operator across its network of operational bases in the Midwest and Southeast of Brazil
04.
Multiple growth avenues
Geographic expansion, fleet electrification, technology-enabled services, and consolidation opportunities in a fragmented market create a clear and executable path for continued value creation

# Growth avenues
Geographic expansion, consolidation opportunities, and fleet electrification position Mobi for a continued value creation trajectory

![](Imagem115.jpg)

Mobi’s growth is expected to be driven by 3 main avenues, to be validated with management

A
B
C
Deepening client wallet share and geographic expansion
Fleet electrification and technology-enabled services
Consolidation of a fragmented market
Well-positioned to deepen wallet share with existing clients while capturing contracts with new clients
Expansion within the existing Goiás-DF-Minas Gerais corridor, and selectively into adjacent regions, leverages existing operational bases and client relationships
The Brazilian corporate transportation market remains fragmented, with consolidation already underway among larger operators in adjacent segments
Mobi’s scale and operational track record position it as a natural consolidation platform for smaller regional fretamento operators
Gradual fleet electrification responds to clients’ ESG and Scope-3 emission-reduction commitments, while a technology layer (route optimization, booking apps, carbon dashboards) can strengthen client stickiness
Formal driver capacitation and retention programs address the sector’s structural driver shortage, supporting fleet growth without a labor bottleneck
